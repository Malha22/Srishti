About the Team

MACE
Team Srishti
20+ Engineers · Various Dept · Kothamangalam, Kerala

Team Srishti is the official technical student team of Mar Athanasius College of Engineering, Kothamangalam, Kerala. The name Srishti — meaning creation in Sanskrit — reflects our core belief: that every problem deserves a purpose-built solution.

Founded by a group of passionate students, Srishti brings together hardware designers, firmware developers, and IoT architects who share one goal — bridging the gap between academic theory and real-world impact through embedded systems, smart sensors, and intelligent edge devices.

With the motto "Build. Iterate. Deploy.", we participate in national hackathons, rural development challenges, and inter-college technical fests — always shipping working prototypes, never just slides.

Vision & Mission

Team Srishti aims to design and develop reliable, high-impact embedded and IoT systems through practical engineering, disciplined iteration, and continuous learning. Our vision is to become a benchmark for student-led hardware innovation in Kerala — inspiring the next wave of engineers to build things that work in the real world.

We operate across five technical domains — Embedded Systems, IoT & Cloud, TinyML & Edge AI, PCB Design, and Agricultural Technology — enabling cross-domain collaboration that produces holistic solutions rather than isolated prototypes.
